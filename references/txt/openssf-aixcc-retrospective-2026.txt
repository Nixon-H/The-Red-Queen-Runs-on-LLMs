## Verified claims

1. Prize pool: "cash prizes from a pot of $30,500,000 were distributed."
   DISCREPANCY FLAG: spec-corpus known-good figure was "~$29.5M total" (announced Black Hat
   Aug 2023). Reconcile against DARPA primary. [TO-VERIFY]

2. Structure: announced 2023, two-year competition, with ARPA-H; support from Anthropic, Google,
   Microsoft, OpenAI; consulting by Linux Foundation/OpenSSF.
   Semifinal (ASC): "42 team submissions ... across two tracks" (Open + Small Business).
   "top seven teams moved forward to the AFC which was set up to mimic a real world CI/CD
   pipeline." => 7 finalists CONFIRMED.

3. Challenge design: real OSS projects forked with ARTIFICIAL bugs inserted; CRSs also found
   REAL bugs during execution ("discovered several real potential bugs alongside the artificial
   ones") -> triage problem delegated to OSTIF -> Ada Logics verification.

4. Real-bug yield: "Ada Logics team was able to reproduce and confirm twenty-seven issues";
   "Teams discovered twenty-seven candidate real-world issues during the competition and OSTIF
   engineers were ultimately able to replicate all of the draft bugs."
   Affected projects: cURL, shadowsocks-libev, healthcare-data-harmonization, hertzbeat,
   little-cms, mongoose.
   Outcome noise datum: some findings already fixed in interim, some outside project threat
   models (Apache POI example), one already known to OSS-Fuzz. -> V5 outcome-definition noise
   exemplar for §9.

5. POST-COMPETITION REDEPLOYMENT (B4 loop evidence, strongest section):
   - FuzzingBrain: "discovering sixty-two vulnerabilities across twenty-six projects, from CUPS
     and Apache Avro to Ghidra and OpenLDAP, with forty-three confirmed by maintainers and
     thirty-six already patched upstream."
   - 42-b3yond-6ug: "twelve kernel-related vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel and related
     components, plus ten zero-day vulnerabilities in userspace projects including Eclipse
     Mosquitto and OpenLDAP."
   - OSS-CRS (Team Atlanta): "twenty-five vulnerabilities across sixteen projects spanning ...
     PHP, U-Boot, memcached, and Apache Ignite 3. Of those, nine have been fixed and eight more
     have been confirmed with fixes in progress."
   - Governance continuation: OpenSSF Cyber Reasoning Systems SIG; OSS-CRS + FuzzingBrain hosted
     at OpenSSF/Linux Foundation.
