# Generative-AI Use Disclosure (for HotCRP "Generative AI usage considerations" field)
Generative AI tools were used in preparing this submission for literature search assistance,
source-format conversion, drafting support, and consistency checking, operating under human
direction with human-reviewed outputs. All factual claims, quotations, and quantitative
statements were verified against primary sources by the authors; all taxonomy classification
decisions are human-approved and published with per-record behavioral evidence in the
artifact. The authors motivate this use as accelerating corpus construction and cross-checking
while retaining full human responsibility for content, per the CFP's generative-AI disclosure
requirement.
